about

Hi, I'm Phoenix. I'm a 16-year-old student in 11th grade. While studying for my school exams, I spend my free time building neural networks and learning how they work under the hood.

I want to understand the math behind machine learning and make models that can run on simple, cheap devices instead of expensive cloud servers.
